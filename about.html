OUR OBJECTIVE

Make collaboration
easier to begin.

Students often have ideas, skills and energy, but finding the right project or teammate can take time. TCEMate brings those starting points into one shared place.

The portal supports the practical work around collaboration: publishing a project, expressing interest, reviewing applicants, sharing discussions and keeping track of updates.

A campus space where students can find one another through the work they want to do.
